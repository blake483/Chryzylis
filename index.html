Our Focus

Where we actively
acquire

We concentrate on essential community healthcare — businesses that serve people at critical moments in their lives, generating stable, recurring revenues with clear succession dynamics.

01
Domiciliary Care

Acquiring established care providers with strong council relationships and stable care teams. We understand that recruitment, retention and culture are central to delivering high-quality care at scale.

02
Dental Practices

Targeting established practices led by principals approaching retirement. We protect patient continuity, clinical culture, and staff retention — building trust rather than disrupting it.

03
Independent Opticians

Acquiring independent optician practices where long-standing patient relationships create durable value. We see significant consolidation opportunity across the UK's fragmented independent sector.

04
Other Sectors — Opportunistic

We remain open to acquisitions outside our core focus where the fundamentals align: 5+ years trading, consistent profitability, a stable team, and an owner ready for their next chapter. If that sounds like your business, we'd value a conversation.

How It Works

A process built around you

From first conversation to completed deal, we move at a pace that works for you — with full transparency at every stage.

Step 01
Initial Conversation

A confidential, no-obligation call with Blake. We want to understand your business, your timeline, and what matters most to you.

Step 02
Indicative Offer

We move quickly. Following our initial review, we'll provide a clear indicative valuation and proposed deal structure — no delays, no ambiguity.

Step 03
Due Diligence

A thorough but respectful process. Our advisory team handles financial, legal, and operational review with minimal disruption to your day-to-day.

Step 04
Completion & Handover

A clean, professionally managed completion. We work with your team to ensure continuity, and we're with you through the transition.

What We Stand For

Our mission
& values

Every acquisition is guided by a consistent set of principles — because how we treat people matters as much as the deal itself.

Legacy Preservation

We honour what you've built — your culture, values, and reputation remain protected. Your business stays in safe, committed hands.

People First

Employees, patients, and communities come before financial engineering. Retaining talent and fostering a positive culture is non-negotiable.

Long-Term Stewardship

We invest for decades, not years. Our 20-year horizon means we focus on sustainable operations rather than short-term extraction.

Integrity & Trust

Transparency throughout every stage. We say what we mean, move at pace, and never leave a seller in the dark.

Empowering Growth

Operational expertise and strategic insight to help businesses scale — while preserving the identity and strengths that made them successful.

Relatable Partnership

We've built and run businesses ourselves. We understand pressure, uncertainty, and what it means to truly care about outcomes — for staff and owners alike.

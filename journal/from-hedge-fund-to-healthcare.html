Journey 2024 · Origins

From hedge fund to healthcare: how Chryzylis began

Blake started building businesses in 2017, his first venture was building a hedge fund focused in and around digital assets. It was rigorous, and analytical work, but also was fundamentally about people and operations.

Understanding our investors needs, goals and ambitions was integral to ensure their motivations were aligned with what we were building.

My next project was to help scale a recruitment business from the ground up. This has taught me something no spreadsheet could; How culture compounds, how retention matters and butterfly effect plays in real life.

Why community healthcare

As funny as it sounds, hedge funds and healthcare are not all too different. The two most important things in people’s life are their health and their finances. Both have to be respected and handled with care. Supporting people through pivotal life events, is something that resonates with me and is what drew me to this sector.

Where Healthcare differs is it is structurally fragmented and owned by founders who have built deep, trusted relationships with almost no institutional support behind them.

The combination of an essential service, and founders nearing succession decisions without an obvious next step, is exactly where Chryzylis was built to operate. Domiciliary care, opticians, and the wider health sector all share this pattern.

"I didn't want to build a fund that extracts value from healthcare. I wanted to build a firm that protects it." — Blake

Where we are now

Chryzylis operates today with that same operator's mindset at its core, supported by an advisory bench across finance, law and operations, and guided by a twenty-year investment philosophy.

Every acquisition we make will be reinforced by the same conviction: the businesses that matter most to their communities deserve owners who will protect, not just profit from, what's been built.

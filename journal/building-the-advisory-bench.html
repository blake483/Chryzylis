Journey 2024 · Building Chryzylis

Building the advisory bench: finance, law and operations

A transaction is only as good as the people executing it. Early on, we made a deliberate decision: rather than building a large in-house deal team, we would assemble a bench of specialist advisors who could bring depth exactly where it was needed, on every deal.

Churchill Taxation — accounting and finance

Churchill Taxation leads our financial due diligence and tax structuring on every acquisition. Their rigour gives sellers confidence that the numbers behind any offer have been tested properly, and gives us the clarity we need to structure deals fairly.

Ellis Jones Solicitors — legal

Ellis Jones is our primary legal partner for transaction work. For deals with particular sector complexity — a CQC-regulated care provider, for instance, or a dental practice with NHS contracts — we draw on a wider panel of specialist firms to ensure nothing is missed.

A network built on relationships, not retainers

Beyond our core advisory partners, Blake's professional network includes contacts from DRW, Goldman Sachs and KPMG who provide operational and strategic input across the portfolio. These aren't transactional relationships — they're people who understand the long-term mission behind Chryzylis and who care about the outcomes for the businesses we acquire.

"We don't outsource diligence to tick a box. We build a team for every deal that's as invested in getting it right as we are." — Blake

This structure means every seller gets the benefit of institutional-grade financial and legal scrutiny, delivered with the personal attention of a much smaller firm. It's part of how we keep transactions both thorough and fast.

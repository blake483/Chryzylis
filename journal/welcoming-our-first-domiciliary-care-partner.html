Transaction 2025 · Domiciliary Care

Welcoming our first domiciliary care partner to Chryzylis

Every acquisition begins with a relationship, not a spreadsheet. Our first domiciliary care partnership was no different. It grew out of more than a year of conversations with a founder who had spent over fifteen years building a care provider trusted by their local council and, more importantly, by the families who relied on it.

He wasn't looking for the highest bidder. He was looking for someone who would keep their care teams intact, honour the standards he'd spent a career building, and treat his staff; many of whom had been with the firm for over a decade.

What made this the right partnership

The business had everything we look for in domiciliary care: a stable, well-trained care team, strong local authority relationships built on consistent delivery, and a founder who cared as much about his legacy as his exit. There was no urgency to sell, no distress in the business, only a clear-eyed decision that it was time for the next chapter.

"I didn't want to sell to someone who saw a spreadsheet. I wanted someone who saw what we'd built." — John on his decision to partner with Chryzylis

That sentiment is exactly why Chryzylis exists. Blake's own experience operating and scaling businesses gave him a credibility in these conversations that most acquirers simply don't have. He understands rota pressures, recruitment challenges, and the emotional weight of running a sensitive business, because he has lived them.

What happens next

The care teams remain unchanged and growing steadily. The registered manager remains in post. The founder has stayed on in an advisory capacity through the transition, ensuring continuity for staff, families, and the local authority alike. This is what long-term ownership looks like in practic. Not a sellout figure, but a quiet continuation of the standard of care this business has always provided.

If you're a domiciliary care provider thinking about your next chapter, we'd welcome a confidential conversation — acquisitions@chryzylis.com.
